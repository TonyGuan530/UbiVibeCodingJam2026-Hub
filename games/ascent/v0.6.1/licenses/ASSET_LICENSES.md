# Asset licensing, origin and source distinction

1. `assets/fallback/**`: original procedurally generated low-poly props/characters, 10 UI SVG glyphs and 10 synthesized WAV sounds made for this demo. Dedicated to the public domain under the **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal** waiver: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . The scripts that generate them are source code under MIT.
2. `prototype/**`, `tools/**`, `qa/**`: demo implementation and helper scripts licensed under MIT (`LICENSE_CODE_MIT.txt`).
3. `assets/asset_registry.json`: records third-party official source packs which are **not embedded in this release**. If downloaded, track each publisher and their stated CC0 license. Listed titles and logos belong to their respective creators; do not treat Kenney brand marks as downloadable game logo assets.
4. Kenney asset license FAQ (CC0, no required attribution): https://kenney.nl/support .
5. Quaternius source examples and license markers: https://quaternius.com/packs/zombieapocalypsekit.html and https://quaternius.com/packs/modularscifimegakit.html . Only the website's free edition is referenced by the registry; paid Source packs are not required.

The inclusion of download links does not mean the linked original ZIP files have been downloaded, scanned, imported, or repackaged here. If your organization needs a third-party attribution file, copy the records from `assets/asset_registry.json` and add the names of files actually imported.
